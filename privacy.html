Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Applies to HEIC Photo Viewer & Converter for Windows, published by Yvan Ngneunmeu

The short version: the app collects no personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app makes no internet connections of its own. Your photos never leave your PC.

Your photos

The app opens, displays and converts photos entirely on your computer. It reads only the files you open, drop into it, or choose to convert. It never uploads them anywhere.

Your original photos are never changed by viewing or converting. The app writes to your files only when you ask it to, for example when you save a converted copy or choose Delete on a photo.

Photos often contain details such as the camera model, the date and the location where they were taken. The app shows these details to you and does not send them anywhere. When converting, you can keep these details, remove the location (the default), or remove all details from the copies.

What the app stores on your PC

To work properly, the app keeps a few files in its own private folder on your computer:

  • Settings, such as your theme, language and default conversion options.
  • A local history of your conversions and recently opened photos and folders (file names, folders, formats, sizes and dates), used for the Recent list and the Activity page.
  • A thumbnail cache of small previews, so browsing is fast.
  • A diagnostic log of technical events and errors, which helps troubleshoot problems. It can include file names or folder paths. It never includes the contents of your photos.

None of this is sent to the publisher or to anyone else. You stay in control:

  • Settings → Privacy & storage lets you clear recent items, turn off Remember recent items, clear the thumbnail cache, and open the app data folder.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this folder and everything in it.

File Explorer thumbnails

When the app is your default app for HEIC photos, it also draws their thumbnails in File Explorer. This happens locally, inside Windows, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Internet connections

The app itself doesn't connect to the internet. Two features open a page outside the app, and only when you click them:

  • View on map opens the photo's location on OpenStreetMap in your web browser. The coordinates are then handled by OpenStreetMap under its privacy policy.
  • Rate in Microsoft Store opens the app's Store page or rating window.

Microsoft Store and Windows

The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Downloads, purchases, updates, ratings and reviews are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. If your Windows diagnostic settings allow it, Windows may send crash reports to Microsoft.

Through Microsoft Partner Center, the publisher can see aggregated reports such as the number of installs, ratings and reviews, and crash statistics collected by Windows. These reports never include your photos and don't identify you.

Sharing and selling

The publisher does not collect your personal data, so there is nothing to share or sell.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that reduces your privacy would be clearly described in the app's release notes.
