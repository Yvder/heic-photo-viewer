Frequently asked questions

Which files can the app open?

HEIC and HEIF photos from iPhones and modern cameras (.heic, .heif, .hif, and the burst formats .heics and .heifs), plus AVIF. It also opens JPG, PNG, WebP, JPEG XL, TIFF, BMP and GIF images.

Do I need to install the HEVC or HEIF extensions from Microsoft?

No. The app has its own decoder, so HEIC photos open without any extra codecs, including the paid HEVC Video Extensions.

How do I convert photos to JPG, PNG or another format?

Open the Convert page from the left bar. Drop photos or whole folders onto it, or use Browse photos / Choose folder. Then choose:

  • Format: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, TIFF, BMP or GIF.
  • Quality and size.
  • Photo details: keep everything, remove the location (recommended), or remove all details.
  • Where to save, and what to do if a file with the same name already exists.

Your originals are never changed. To convert a single photo you're viewing, use the save button at the top right, or press Ctrl+S.

How do I open HEIC photos with a double-click?

In the app, go to Settings → Windows → Make HEIC Photo Viewer & Converter your default photo app for HEIC. Windows Settings opens. Select .heic (and .heif if you like) and choose HEIC Photo Viewer & Converter.

Why don't I see previews (thumbnails) of my HEIC photos in File Explorer?

The app draws HEIC thumbnails in File Explorer when it's your default app for HEIC photos (see the previous question). Make sure File Explorer is set to show medium, large or extra-large icons.

Explorer remembers thumbnails it made before. If some photos still show old icons, clear them: open Disk Cleanup from the Start menu, tick Thumbnails, and select OK.

A photo won't open. What can I do?

Select Try again. If it still fails, the file may be damaged, incomplete (for example, still syncing from the cloud), or use a variant of the format the app doesn't support yet. Please email it, if you're comfortable sharing it, so it can be fixed.

Does the app upload my photos or track me?

No. Everything happens on your PC, and the app makes no internet connections of its own. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where does the app keep its data, and how do I reset it?

Settings, recent items, conversion history and the thumbnail cache live in the app's private folder. Settings → Privacy & storage lets you clear recent items or the thumbnail cache, and open the folder. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11, on an x64 or ARM64 PC.
